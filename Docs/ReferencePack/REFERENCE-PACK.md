# Hellfall: Earth Reclaimed — Visual Reference Pack

This pack defines an original visual direction for the game. It must not reproduce Doom, Doom II, Quake, or any other copyrighted game assets, characters, weapons, maps, logos, or artwork.

## Global visual direction

Modern 2026 premium retro-FPS, Unreal Engine 5.8.3, high-detail 3D concept art, 4K PBR-ready surfaces, Nanite-scale geometry, Lumen lighting, readable silhouettes, bold color blocking, brutalist industrial architecture, biomechanical alien technology, cinematic but gameplay-readable composition, no text, no logos, no watermark.

## Required concept images

### 01 — Environment: Ruined Megacity

Wide gameplay concept of a destroyed megacity conversion zone at dusk: brutalist towers, collapsed skybridges, neon cyan emergency lights, toxic yellow hazard markings, magenta dimensional fractures, wet concrete, rusted steel, smoke and volumetric fog, multiple combat routes and readable cover, modern 2026 AAA game quality, original design.

### 02 — Environment: Industrial Forge

Large industrial weapons foundry with molten energy channels, suspended machinery, heavy steel platforms, maintenance bridges, dramatic orange and cyan lighting, modular arena layout, climbable elevation, cover, doors and combat lanes, detailed PBR materials, original science-fiction design.

### 03 — Environment: Orbital Station

Interior of an orbital military station with rotating habitat structures, damaged windows overlooking Earth, low-gravity combat arena, clean futuristic panels mixed with emergency damage, cyan guide lights and red warning lights, strong depth and gameplay readability, modern Unreal Engine concept art.

### 04 — Environment: Interdimensional Realm

Surreal alien citadel with non-Euclidean architecture, floating brutalist platforms, biomechanical surfaces, glowing energy veins, impossible geometry used as a gameplay space, deep black voids, cyan and magenta portal light, highly detailed but readable, original visual language.

### 05 — Weapon: Valkyrie Arc-Carbine

Professional weapon design sheet of an original futuristic automatic energy rifle, compact industrial construction, replaceable arc-cell power unit, heat vents, worn ceramic armor, brushed metal, cyan energy core, front/side/three-quarter views, neutral dark background, no text or logos.

### 06 — Weapon: Singularity Disruptor

Original heavy superweapon concept, large black-hole energy device with a reinforced industrial frame, magnetic rings, restrained cyan-violet emission, dangerous but believable engineering, front/side/three-quarter views, high-detail 3D render, no text or logos.

### 07 — Enemy: Razor-Stalker

Original quadrupedal biomechanical melee creature, fast athletic silhouette, segmented armor, exposed energy tendons, sharp but distinct anatomy, designed for clear gameplay readability, front/side/three-quarter views, neutral studio lighting, no resemblance to existing game monsters.

### 08 — Enemy: Phalanx Trooper

Original armored interdimensional infantry unit with a frontal energy shield, asymmetrical industrial armor, compact energy weapon, readable weak points, cyan shield glow and toxic yellow markings, front/side/three-quarter views, no logos or text.

### 09 — Material Board

Material reference board showing original 4K PBR-ready surfaces: wet concrete, rusted steel, painted hazard panels, alien biomechanical tissue, emissive energy veins, scratched ceramic armor, glass, cables, grime decals and damaged metal. Include albedo, roughness, metallic and normal-map style swatches without written labels.

### 10 — Lighting and Color Script

Six cinematic gameplay lighting thumbnails for the four campaign environments: cold cyan urban dusk, toxic yellow industrial interior, red emergency orbital station, magenta interdimensional citadel, plus neutral combat readability and boss encounter lighting. Strong contrast, bold colors, clear player navigation.

### 11 — HUD Direction

Original modern FPS HUD concept: minimal health, armor, ammo, crosshair, weapon indicator, objective marker and damage feedback. Dark translucent panels, cyan and amber accents, readable at high speed, scalable for 4K and ultrawide displays, no existing game UI references, no text except abstract placeholder shapes.

### 12 — Key Art

Original cinematic key art for Hellfall: Earth Reclaimed: lone armored operative standing before a fractured city gateway, ruined megacity and alien geometry merging behind them, cyan, magenta and toxic yellow palette, industrial metal atmosphere, premium 2026 game visual quality, dramatic wide composition, no logo, no title, no text.

## Reusable negative prompt

Avoid copyrighted characters, Doom-like demons, recognizable Doom weapons, Doom level layouts, existing game logos, skull motifs copied from other franchises, generic fantasy armor, medieval weapons, blurry textures, low-poly assets, unreadable silhouettes, excessive visual noise, text, watermark, signature, UI clutter, and direct imitation of any existing game.

## Intended use

These images are visual direction references only. They are not final production assets. Final game assets must be modeled, textured, licensed, or generated separately and validated for commercial use.
