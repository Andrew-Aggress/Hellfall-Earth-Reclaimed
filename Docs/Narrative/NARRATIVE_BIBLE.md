# Hellfall: Earth Reclaimed — Narrative Bible

## Core promise

Humanity did not lose Earth in a single battle. It lost it one system at a time: power, communications, transport, orbital defense, then memory. The player fights to restore those systems while discovering that the invasion is not simply an occupation. Earth is being converted into a machine capable of opening a permanent gate between realities.

## Tone

Fast, violent, urgent, and atmospheric. The story is delivered in short radio exchanges, environmental storytelling, mission objectives, damaged recordings, and rare cinematic moments. Dialogue must respect the pace of an arena FPS: concise lines, strong verbs, no long exposition during combat.

## Player character

**Operative Jaxen “Rook” Thorne** — former Aegis Vanguard breach specialist. Rook survived the collapse of the orbital defense network and spent three years trapped in a sealed transit bunker. Rook is disciplined, observant, and dryly humorous, but carries guilt over abandoning an evacuation convoy.

Rook is not a chosen one. Rook is a trained operative with incomplete information, limited time, and a practical objective: destroy the conversion network before Earth becomes permanently uninhabitable.

## Main antagonist

**Empress Xylia** — leader of the Vespera Concordat. Xylia does not see herself as a conqueror. She believes unstable realities inevitably collapse and that Earth can be transformed into an anchor to save billions of lives in her own dimension. Her logic is coherent, but her solution treats human life as raw material.

## Supporting characters

### Mara Venn — Aegis command intelligence

A fragmented military intelligence reconstructed from surviving satellites. Mara is precise, protective, and increasingly self-aware. She begins by treating Rook as an asset and gradually recognizes Rook as a person.

### Dr. Elias Soren — conversion scientist

A human scientist forced to work for the Concordat. His research made the Earth-anchor possible. He sends hidden warnings through maintenance systems and becomes an uneasy ally.

### Commander Vael — Concordat field commander

Xylia’s most effective human-facing commander. Vael believes resistance is mathematically pointless and uses efficiency, not cruelty, to control the occupied zones. He becomes the first major boss.

### The Architect

An ancient Concordat intelligence responsible for converting physical space into stable gate structures. It has no conventional body and uses buildings, machines, and enemy units as extensions of its mind.

## Central themes

- Survival versus surrender.
- The cost of turning people into systems.
- Whether a damaged world can be reclaimed rather than merely preserved.
- The difference between understanding an enemy and forgiving it.

## Dialogue rules

- Combat barks: 3–12 words.
- Mission transmissions: usually under 45 seconds.
- No jokes during deaths, civilian losses, or major reveals.
- Rook speaks rarely, but every line should reveal intent or character.
- Mara uses operational language early and more personal language later.
- Xylia never shouts; calm certainty makes her more threatening.
- Avoid direct references to real-world politics, religions, or existing game franchises.
