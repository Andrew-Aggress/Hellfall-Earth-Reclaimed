# Prologue and E1M1 Dialogue

## Opening cinematic — “The Last Broadcast”

**[Black screen. A damaged emergency tone repeats twice.]**

**MARA:** Aegis emergency channel. Authentication failed. Repeating civilian evacuation order.

**[Static. A distant impact. The sound of a city losing power.]**

**MARA:** Evacuation corridor seven is no longer operational. Corridor eight is no longer operational.

**[A sealed bunker door unlocks.]**

**MARA:** Operative Thorne. If you can hear this, you are the last active Aegis signature in the continental network.

**ROOK:** How long?

**MARA:** Three years, eleven months, and six days.

**ROOK:** Then we are late.

**MARA:** We are not late. We are the remaining option.

## E1M1 — Breach Point

### Mission briefing

**MARA:** The bunker’s upper levels are occupied. Your route exits through the old subway maintenance line.

**ROOK:** Any friendly signals?

**MARA:** One. It stopped transmitting seven minutes ago.

**ROOK:** Then we move before they find the source.

### First weapon pickup

**MARA:** Valkyrie Arc-Carbine. Prototype issue. Ammunition: arc cells.

**ROOK:** Still works?

**MARA:** It has been waiting longer than you have.

### First enemy encounter

**MARA:** Motion ahead. Four contacts. No human heat signatures.

**ROOK:** Good.

**MARA:** That is not the word I would choose.

### First arena locked-door event

**MARA:** The transit gate is sealed. Concordat control node nearby.

**ROOK:** I see it.

**MARA:** You see a machine. I see the network behind it.

**ROOK:** Then break the network.

### Secret area discovery

**MARA:** Hidden maintenance chamber detected. Aegis markings are still active.

**ROOK:** Someone kept it alive.

**MARA:** Or someone wanted to be remembered.

### Level completion

**[The subway gate opens. The ruined city glows beyond the tunnel.]**

**MARA:** Surface air is contaminated but breathable.

**ROOK:** And the city?

**MARA:** Converting.

**ROOK:** Then we interrupt it.
