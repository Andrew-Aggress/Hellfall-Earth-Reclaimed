# E1M1 — Breach Point

## Mission summary

Rook wakes inside a sealed Aegis transit bunker after nearly four years of isolation. The bunker has been found by the Concordat. Rook must recover the Valkyrie Arc-Carbine, cross the maintenance tunnels, restore power to the subway gate, and reach the surface.

## Gameplay objectives

1. Exit the recovery chamber.
2. Recover the Valkyrie Arc-Carbine.
3. Eliminate the first Razor-Stalker group.
4. Restore power to the transit gate.
5. Explore the hidden Aegis maintenance chamber.
6. Survive the locked-door arena.
7. Open the subway gate.
8. Reach the surface.

## Arena design

- Spawn room: quiet tutorial space with movement prompts.
- Maintenance corridor: first weapon pickup and first enemy reveal.
- Power junction: small combat arena with two routes and vertical cover.
- Security platform: elevated combat space with a locked gate.
- Secret chamber: optional lore recording and ammunition.
- Subway gate: final arena with escalating enemy waves.

## Environmental storytelling

- Evacuation posters are faded and partially covered by alien growth.
- A wall display shows the same emergency message repeating for years.
- Aegis helmets are arranged beside an unopened evacuation door.
- The first visible dimensional growth follows power cables toward the surface.

## Completion reward

Unlocks the next mission: **E1M2 — Neon Slums**.

The player receives the first campaign record:

> “The city is not dead. It is being used.”
