# Episode 1 — Ruined Megacity Missions

## E1M1 — Breach Point

Rook escapes the buried Aegis bunker, recovers the Valkyrie Arc-Carbine, and reaches the surface. The player learns that the city is being converted rather than simply destroyed.

## E1M2 — Neon Slums

Rook crosses a vertical residential district where emergency signs still direct civilians toward evacuation routes. Concordat growth has occupied the towers and turned the streets into a power-harvesting grid.

Gameplay focus:

- vertical movement;
- first secret rooms;
- rooftop routes;
- Razor-Stalker ambushes;
- first health and armor pickups.

Key transmission:

**MARA:** The district’s power draw is increasing.

**ROOK:** There is no city power left.

**MARA:** Correct. The district is drawing power from the people still inside it.

## E1M3 — Monorail Yards

Rook reaches a rail depot and uses an automated maintenance train to cross the quarantine perimeter. The train network is still active, but the Concordat has converted the tracks into a moving defense system.

Gameplay focus:

- moving platforms;
- train hazards;
- open combat lanes;
- timed switches;
- first shielded Phalanx Troopers.

Key event:

The player must board a moving train while enemies attack from adjacent platforms. The train arrives at the military perimeter with only seconds before the bridge collapses.

## E1M4 — Corporate Plaza

The old financial district has become a Concordat observation zone. Glass towers provide sniper sightlines while conversion pylons distort the plaza’s geometry.

Gameplay focus:

- long sightlines;
- sniper pressure;
- destructible glass;
- multi-level plaza combat;
- optional route through a collapsed office tower.

Key transmission:

**MARA:** The pylons are mapping human movement.

**ROOK:** Let them map mine.

**MARA:** I recommend not standing still.

## E1M5 — Vael’s Spire

Rook climbs the communications tower and confronts Commander Vael. The tower is both a military command center and a conversion antenna. Vael uses the building’s defenses as extensions of his armor.

Boss: **Sub-Commander Vael**

Phases:

1. Armored ranged combat across the communications floor.
2. Teleportation between signal towers.
3. Arena collapse and close-range pursuit.

Boss dialogue:

**VAEL:** You are not reclaiming Earth. You are delaying its purpose.

**ROOK:** Then why are you still trying to stop me?

**VAEL:** Because delay is the only form of mercy your species has earned.

After the battle, Vael’s final transmission reveals that the Concordat’s central gate is not in orbit. It is beneath the old defense belt.

## E1M6 — Quarantine Line

Rook descends through a military quarantine sector where human survivors were separated from infected conversion zones. The quarantine system is still enforcing orders, but its identification system has been rewritten.

Gameplay focus:

- locked checkpoints;
- automated turrets;
- mixed human and Concordat technology;
- rescue signals that may be traps.

Story beat:

Rook finds evidence that Aegis command knew about the conversion process before the official evacuation order.

## E1M7 — The Flooded Exchange

A collapsed underground market has filled with chemically contaminated water and alien growth. Rook must reroute power through maintenance tunnels while fighting in partially flooded spaces.

Gameplay focus:

- environmental hazards;
- electrical water zones;
- hidden maintenance routes;
- support enemies that deploy Nanite Pods.

Key transmission:

**MARA:** I found an archived Aegis command packet.

**ROOK:** Open it.

**MARA:** It contains your name.

**ROOK:** Open it.

## E1M8 — Outskirts Relay

Rook reaches the first Aegis relay outside the city and restores the regional defense network. The relay exposes the location of the underground gate component factory in Episode 2.

Gameplay focus:

- large outdoor arena;
- all Episode 1 enemy types;
- relay defense event;
- resource management;
- final transition to the military sector.

Mission ending:

**MARA:** Regional relay restored. I can see the defense belt.

**ROOK:** How bad?

**MARA:** It is not a belt anymore. It is a factory.

**ROOK:** Then we shut it down.

## Episode 1 narrative reward

At the end of the episode, the player unlocks the recovered Aegis file **“Operation Anchor”**. It reveals that the first dimensional signal was detected before the invasion and that someone inside Aegis authorized the original gate experiment.
