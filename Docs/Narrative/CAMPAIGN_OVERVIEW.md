# Campaign Overview

## Episode 1 — Ruined Megacity

Earth’s surface is divided into Conversion Zones. Rook escapes a buried transit bunker, crosses the ruined city, and reactivates the first Aegis relay. The episode ends with Commander Vael revealing that the invasion began three years earlier than the official emergency broadcast claimed.

## Episode 2 — Military and Industrial Sector

Rook reaches the old defense belt and discovers that human factories are producing Concordat gate components. Dr. Soren guides Rook through the industrial network. The Architect awakens beneath the foundry and begins rearranging the facility into a living gate.

## Episode 3 — Orbital Port and Space Station

Rook launches through the orbital tether and enters a damaged military station. In zero gravity, Rook learns that Earth’s moon is being used as a stabilizer. The Leviathan Core, an orbital weapon turned into a gate engine, must be disabled from inside its firing chamber.

## Episode 4 — Interdimensional Realm

Rook crosses the completed gate and enters the Concordat’s reality. Xylia offers a temporary alliance: Earth can survive if humanity accepts conversion. Rook rejects the bargain, destroys the anchor network, and forces the two realities apart. The ending leaves Earth damaged but free, with a final signal suggesting one fragment of the gate survived.

## Ending direction

The ending is hopeful but not clean. Earth is not restored instantly. Cities remain damaged, orbital infrastructure is broken, and millions are missing. The victory is that humanity retains agency and the future is no longer predetermined by the Concordat.
