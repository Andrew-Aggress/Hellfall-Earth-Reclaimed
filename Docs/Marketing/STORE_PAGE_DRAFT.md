# Hellfall: Earth Reclaimed — Store Page Draft

## Short description

Fight through a converted Earth in a fast, brutal, original arena FPS built for modern hardware. Master momentum, weapon variety, secrets, and high-pressure combat across ruined cities, industrial megastructures, orbital stations, and fractured realities.

## Long description

Earth is not being destroyed. It is being converted.

Hellfall: Earth Reclaimed is a high-speed single-player and cooperative FPS about momentum, aggression, exploration, and survival. Fight through massive combat arenas, uncover hidden routes, recover lost Aegis technology, and stop an interdimensional empire from turning the planet into a permanent gateway.

### Features

- fast first-person movement with jumping, air control, and sliding;
- original arsenal with distinct tactical roles;
- aggressive enemies with readable behaviors and weaknesses;
- 32-level campaign across four radically different episodes;
- secrets, lore records, weapon upgrades, and New Game Plus;
- 2–4 player cooperative campaign planned;
- modern Unreal Engine visuals with high-resolution materials and dynamic lighting;
- English, Ukrainian, Russian, German, Polish, French, and Italian localization;
- survival arena and challenge modes.
