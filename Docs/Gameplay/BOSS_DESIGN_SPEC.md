# Boss Design Specification

## Boss design principles

- Every boss has a readable silhouette and attack language.
- Each phase introduces one major new rule, not several confusing rules.
- Boss arenas must support movement, recovery, and multiple combat routes.
- Dialogue must never hide a critical attack cue.
- Every boss has a visible vulnerability window.
- Multiplayer scaling changes adds and timing before simply increasing health.

## Sub-Commander Vael

Role: mobile ranged commander.

Weakness: signal towers and exposed teleport anchors.

Counterplay: break line of sight, use vertical movement, damage anchors during teleport recovery.

Arena hazards: collapsing platforms, electrical signal arcs, falling debris.

## The Architect

Role: environment-controller boss.

Weakness: stabilizer nodes and exposed core conduits.

Counterplay: read arena changes, keep moving, use area damage on summoned units.

Arena hazards: moving walls, void zones, rotating platforms.

## Leviathan Core

Role: massive weapon intelligence.

Weakness: cooling arrays and charging core.

Counterplay: move along the barrel, destroy heat vents, use timing windows between discharges.

Arena hazards: rotating barrel segments, energy channels, decompression.

## Empress Xylia

Role: final adaptive boss.

Weakness: anchor nodes and phase transitions.

Counterplay: weapon variety, movement mastery, attention to changing gravity and arena geometry.

Arena hazards: collapsing platforms, reality fractures, temporary inverted gravity.
