# Enemy Roster

## Razor-Stalker

Fast melee pressure unit. Forces movement and punishes predictable routes.

## Aero-Wraith

Floating drone with rapid plasma darts. Moves vertically and attacks from open angles. Weak against sustained automatic fire.

## Phalanx Trooper

Armored shield unit. Strong from the front, vulnerable from the rear and during weapon fire.

## Spark-Caster

Fragile support caster. Creates temporary lightning zones and retreats behind other enemies.

## Overwatch Sentinel

Camouflaged sniper. Controls long sightlines and forces the player to use cover or break line of sight.

## Hive-Weaver

Support unit that deploys Nanite Pods. Priority target because it increases encounter density over time.

## Juggernaut Exarch

Slow elite with a rotary cannon. Back weak point and heavy stagger response.

## Nanite Cloud

Area-denial swarm. Does not chase directly; instead, it closes routes and forces constant relocation.

## Concordat variants

Each base enemy can receive a limited variant for later episodes:

- reinforced armor;
- unstable dimensional core;
- stealth camouflage;
- burning energy state;
- corrupted Aegis equipment.

Variants must change one tactical rule only. Do not create visually indistinguishable stat upgrades.

## Enemy communication

Enemy communication is short and functional. Units may broadcast:

- player location;
- shield state;
- reinforcement request;
- retreat warning;
- boss phase transition.

## Spawn rules

- never spawn an enemy directly in the player’s view without a readable entrance;
- preserve a minimum escape route;
- avoid stacking melee enemies in narrow spaces;
- spawn support units where they can be seen but not immediately reached;
- use sound to warn of off-screen threats.
