# Episode 4 — Interdimensional Realm

## Episode arc

Rook enters the Concordat’s reality and discovers that the invasion is part of a survival program for a collapsing dimension. Xylia offers Earth as a permanent anchor. Rook rejects the exchange and destroys the gate from within, leaving both realities wounded but free from forced conversion.

## New gameplay themes

- shifting level geometry;
- non-Euclidean shortcuts;
- inverted gravity moments;
- reality instability;
- enemy combinations from all previous episodes;
- boss arenas that change between phases;
- high-intensity endgame encounters.

## E4M1 — The Bleed

### Objectives

1. Stabilize Rook’s equipment.
2. Follow the surviving Mara signal.
3. Cross the fractured corridor.
4. Destroy three reality anchors.
5. Reach the Concordat boundary.

### Gameplay focus

- corridors that reconfigure after combat;
- visual landmarks that remain constant;
- portal shortcuts;
- gravity changes communicated by lighting and particles.

## E4M2 — Xylia’s Citadel

Rook enters the imperial citadel. The area is elegant, enormous, and completely built around the preservation of Xylia’s reality.

### Objectives

1. Enter the citadel.
2. Reach the imperial archive.
3. Recover the original anchor design.
4. Survive the citadel guard encounter.
5. Confront Empress Xylia.

## E4M3 — The Nexus

The Nexus connects every converted zone. It is the largest combat space in the campaign and combines enemy types from all episodes.

### Objectives

1. Destroy the city anchor.
2. Destroy the industrial anchor.
3. Destroy the orbital anchor.
4. Defend Mara’s signal bridge.
5. Reach the central throne chamber.

### Gameplay focus

- multi-stage arena;
- enemy combinations;
- optional shortcuts unlocked by previous secrets;
- maximum use of weapon upgrades;
- high-density but readable combat.

## E4M4 — Empress Xylia

### Boss phase 1 — The Diplomat

Xylia fights from a distance and uses controlled energy patterns. She speaks throughout the phase and attempts to convince Rook that resistance is irrational.

### Boss phase 2 — The Anchor

Xylia merges with the central gate structure. The arena gains floating platforms, gravity shifts, and rotating energy lanes.

### Boss phase 3 — The Survivor

The gate collapses. Xylia loses control of the structure and fights directly with a high-speed melee form. The player must destroy the anchor nodes while avoiding the collapsing arena.

### Boss dialogue

**XYLIA:** Your world is dying. I am offering it continuity.

**ROOK:** You are offering it a cage.

**XYLIA:** A cage is still safer than the void.

**ROOK:** Not if someone else owns the lock.

## E4M5 — Nightmare: Neon Slums

New routes, altered enemy waves, unstable gravity pockets, and a remixed pylon defense.

## E4M6 — Nightmare: The Forge

Overheated machinery, reversed conveyor routes, elite enemy combinations, and a timed reactor collapse.

## E4M7 — Nightmare: Zenith Ring

Expanded zero-gravity arena, mixed aerial enemies, and limited oxygen.

## E4M8 — Earth Reclaimed

Final post-campaign challenge level. Rook returns to the surface and destroys the last surviving anchor fragment. The level combines the visual language of all four episodes.

### Final line

**MARA:** The gate is gone.

**ROOK:** All of it?

**MARA:** No.

**ROOK:** Then we are not finished.

**MARA:** We are alive.

**ROOK:** For now, that is enough.
