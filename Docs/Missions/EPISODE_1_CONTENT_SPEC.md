# Episode 1 — Content Specification

## Episode arc

The first episode moves from isolation to awareness. Rook begins alone beneath the city, discovers that the invasion is an active conversion process, confronts the first Concordat commander, and reaches the military sector with proof that the gate network is being built on Earth.

## E1M2 — Neon Slums

### Story purpose

Show the first surface district and establish that civilians may still be alive inside the conversion zones.

### Primary objectives

1. Cross the residential block.
2. Activate the emergency lift.
3. Reach the rooftop relay.
4. Disable the local conversion pylon.
5. Evacuate through the maintenance bridge.

### Optional objective

Find the apartment signal and recover a survivor’s audio log.

### Encounters

- Razor-Stalkers in narrow stairwells;
- rooftop ambush with elevated routes;
- first support unit deploying Nanite Pods;
- final pylon arena with moving cover.

### Radio line

**MARA:** I have a civilian signal in the east tower.

**ROOK:** Can you reach them?

**MARA:** I can hear them. That is not the same thing.

### Reward

Unlocks the first weapon upgrade: reduced spread while sliding.

## E1M3 — Monorail Yards

### Story purpose

Move the player from urban exploration into larger, more kinetic combat spaces.

### Primary objectives

1. Reach the monorail control room.
2. Restore track power.
3. Board the maintenance train.
4. Survive the moving-platform assault.
5. Cross the quarantine bridge.

### Optional objective

Recover three abandoned transit cores to open a shortcut through the depot.

### Encounters

- Phalanx Troopers introduced in pairs;
- enemy attacks from moving train cars;
- destructible glass catwalk;
- timed train departure sequence.

### Radio line

**MARA:** The train has no driver.

**ROOK:** Then it cannot complain about the route.

**MARA:** It is following a route written by the Concordat.

**ROOK:** Then we change the route.

### Reward

Unlocks a shortcut in E1M4 and a new arena score multiplier.

## E1M4 — Corporate Plaza

### Story purpose

Reveal that the Concordat is using human infrastructure and archived data to optimize the conversion process.

### Primary objectives

1. Enter the corporate plaza.
2. Locate the data archive.
3. Disable three observation pylons.
4. Reach the executive tower.
5. Extract the conversion map.

### Optional objective

Destroy the surveillance archive before extracting the map.

### Encounters

- sniper pressure across long sightlines;
- Phalanx Troopers guarding pylon controls;
- close-range ambush inside the archive;
- plaza lockdown with multiple entrances.

### Radio line

**MARA:** The archive contains human evacuation patterns.

**ROOK:** Delete it.

**MARA:** It may contain the location of survivors.

**ROOK:** Copy survivors. Burn the map.

### Reward

Optional completion changes Mara’s later E1M5 dialogue and removes one sniper wave from the return route.

## E1M5 — Vael’s Spire

### Story purpose

Introduce the first major antagonist and reveal that the invasion has a planned end state.

### Primary objectives

1. Enter the communications spire.
2. Disable the external signal towers.
3. Climb to the command floor.
4. Defeat Sub-Commander Vael.
5. Recover the gate coordinates.

### Boss arena

The arena is divided into three connected floors. The player must use vertical movement, break line of sight, and exploit exposed signal relays during Vael’s teleport phases.

### Boss reward

- gate coordinates;
- access to Episode 2;
- weapon upgrade slot;
- first major lore reveal: Operation Anchor.

## E1M6 — Quarantine Line

### Story purpose

Expose the failure of Aegis command and introduce human infrastructure being used against civilians.

### Primary objectives

1. Enter the quarantine command center.
2. Override the identity checkpoint.
3. Disable automated defense turrets.
4. Open the civilian shelter route.
5. Reach the outer defense belt.

### Optional objective

Rescue three trapped civilians. This changes the ambient population and dialogue in E1M8.

### Encounters

- automated turrets and Phalanx Troopers;
- narrow security corridors;
- alarm escalation event;
- optional stealth route that avoids one encounter.

### Radio line

**SURVIVOR:** The quarantine doors locked from the outside.

**ROOK:** Who gave the order?

**SURVIVOR:** The same voice that told us rescue was coming.

## E1M7 — Flooded Exchange

### Story purpose

Give Dr. Soren his first active role and connect the city conversion system to the underground factory network.

### Primary objectives

1. Reach the flooded exchange.
2. Drain the electrical channels.
3. Find Dr. Soren’s hidden lab.
4. Protect the lab while data is transferred.
5. Follow the factory signal underground.

### Optional objective

Recover Soren’s research samples without destroying them.

### Encounters

- water and electrical hazards;
- Nanite Pods spawning Razor-Stalkers;
- laboratory defense event;
- emergency extraction through a collapsing tunnel.

### Dr. Soren line

**SOREN:** The gate is not opening above us.

**ROOK:** Then where?

**SOREN:** Below. Every factory in the defense belt is building one piece of it.

## E1M8 — Outskirts Relay

### Story purpose

Close the city arc and transition the player into the industrial campaign.

### Primary objectives

1. Reach the Aegis relay.
2. Restore relay power.
3. Defend the relay during synchronization.
4. Upload the conversion map.
5. Reach the military transport route.

### Optional objective

Activate the survivor beacon if E1M6 rescue objectives were completed.

### Final encounter

Three combat lanes converge on the relay. Enemy waves increase pressure while the player must rotate between consoles and defense positions. The relay cannot be repaired while the player is downed or the final elite remains active.

### Episode ending

**MARA:** The defense belt is manufacturing gate components.

**ROOK:** How many?

**MARA:** Enough to make the next gate permanent.

**ROOK:** Then we reach the factory before they finish it.

### Unlocks

- Episode 2: Military and Industrial Sector;
- weapon upgrade system;
- campaign record: Operation Anchor;
- optional survivor radio channel.

## Episode 1 consistency rules

- Every level has one primary visual landmark.
- Every level has one optional secret or lore object.
- Radio lines are short enough to hear during combat.
- Main objectives never require a specific language.
- Optional objectives provide context or convenience, never mandatory power.
- All mission names, objective text, dialogue, and rewards use localization keys.
