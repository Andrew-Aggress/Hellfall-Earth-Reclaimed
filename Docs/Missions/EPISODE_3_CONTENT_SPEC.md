# Episode 3 — Orbital Port and Space Station

## Episode arc

Rook reaches orbit through the tether launch system and enters a damaged Aegis station. The station is still transmitting friendly identification codes, but its systems have been partially rewritten by the Concordat. The episode moves from military science fiction into low-gravity and zero-gravity combat, ending with the destruction of the Leviathan Core.

## New gameplay themes

- low-gravity traversal;
- zero-gravity combat arenas;
- 360-degree navigation;
- decompression hazards;
- magnetic surfaces;
- orbital machinery;
- long-range enemy threats;
- limited oxygen in damaged sections.

## E3M1 — Tether Ascent

### Objectives

1. Stabilize the launch capsule.
2. Exit into the tether shaft.
3. Climb through the maintenance rings.
4. Restore artificial gravity.
5. Dock with the orbital port.

### Encounters

- flying Aero-Wraith drones;
- vertical shaft combat;
- moving maintenance platforms;
- damaged gravity generators;
- first exposure to exterior space.

## E3M2 — Zenith Ring

### Objectives

1. Reach the station ring.
2. Restore the navigation core.
3. Cross the zero-gravity arena.
4. Recover the station command key.
5. Open the military hangar.

### Optional objective

Recover the station’s final civilian evacuation roster.

### Gameplay rule

Zero-gravity areas must always provide clear landmarks, directional lighting, and readable traversal anchors. The player should never be lost because the camera lacks an up direction.

## E3M3 — Leviathan Core

### Story purpose

Reveal that the orbital defense cannon has been converted into a gate stabilizer.

### Objectives

1. Enter the Leviathan weapon barrel.
2. Disable the first cooling array.
3. Reach the internal control chamber.
4. Destroy the targeting core.
5. Escape before the cannon fires.

### Boss: Leviathan Core

The player fights inside a colossal orbital cannon. The boss is the cannon’s living control intelligence rather than a traditional creature.

Phases:

1. Internal defense drones and rotating barrel segments.
2. Energy discharge paths that divide the arena.
3. Core exposure during charging cycles.
4. Emergency escape while the cannon tears itself apart.

### Dialogue

**LEVIATHAN:** Aegis authorization confirmed. Fire sequence accepted.

**ROOK:** Authorization revoked.

**LEVIATHAN:** Revocation rejected. Authorization source is deceased.

**ROOK:** Then take it from me.

## E3M4 — Station Blackout

Rook moves through an unpowered station sector where only emergency lights and enemy emissions reveal the route.

Gameplay focus:

- darkness and controlled lighting;
- silent machinery;
- ambush routes;
- emergency power cells;
- optional manual restoration of station systems.

## E3M5 — Docking Graveyard

Rook crosses a field of destroyed ships and station fragments connected by temporary magnetic bridges.

Gameplay focus:

- exterior traversal;
- limited oxygen;
- long-range combat;
- drifting debris;
- optional rescue beacon.

## E3M6 — Gate Assembly

The Concordat is assembling the final orbital anchor. Rook must sabotage the assembly line while fighting on rotating platforms.

Gameplay focus:

- rotating arena geometry;
- moving platform timing;
- heavy enemy squads;
- destruction of three anchor components.

## E3M7 — Command Deck

Rook reaches the old Aegis command deck and discovers that the original gate experiment was initiated by a human command group.

Gameplay focus:

- story-heavy exploration;
- security lockdown;
- command terminals;
- elite enemy guards;
- branching optional dialogue with Mara.

## E3M8 — Dimensional Gate

Rook must stop the Concordat from opening the completed gate. The mission ends with the player entering the gate to prevent the anchor from stabilizing.

### Final event

- destroy four stabilizer nodes;
- survive a rotating defense arena;
- activate the gate manually;
- enter the interdimensional realm before the facility collapses.

### Episode ending

**SOREN:** Once you cross, the gate may not recognize you as human.

**ROOK:** It recognized us well enough to invade.

**MARA:** Rook, I am receiving a signal from inside the gate.

**ROOK:** From who?

**MARA:** From me.
