# Episode 2 — Military and Industrial Sector

## Episode arc

Rook leaves the ruined city and enters the defense belt, where human factories are producing the components of a permanent dimensional gate. Dr. Soren provides technical guidance while Mara uncovers evidence that the original Aegis gate experiment was not destroyed — it was repurposed by the Concordat.

## New gameplay themes

- heavier enemies;
- industrial hazards;
- moving machinery;
- environmental power routing;
- weapon upgrades;
- destructible cover;
- multi-stage combat arenas;
- optional research data and rescue routes.

## E2M1 — Blackwood Armory

### Story purpose

Introduce the defense belt and the first weapon upgrade station.

### Objectives

1. Enter the armory.
2. Recover the armory access key.
3. Activate the weapon calibration station.
4. Clear the loading floor.
5. Reach the factory transit elevator.

### New elements

- weapon upgrade station;
- armored Phalanx variants;
- explosive storage hazards;
- moving cargo platforms.

### Dialogue

**SOREN:** The armory was built to defend Earth.

**ROOK:** It is still doing that.

**SOREN:** The weapons are pointed inward.

**ROOK:** Then we turn them around.

## E2M2 — The Forge

### Story purpose

Show the industrial scale of the gate construction process.

### Objectives

1. Cross the upper foundry.
2. Shut down the molten energy pumps.
3. Redirect the cooling system.
4. Destroy the unfinished gate frame.
5. Escape before the forge overloads.

### New hazards

- molten energy channels;
- rotating machinery;
- timed pressure doors;
- falling platforms;
- heat zones that damage the player over time.

### Optional objective

Recover the original Aegis manufacturing blueprint before the forge collapses.

## E2M3 — Subterranean Silos

### Story purpose

Shift into a claustrophobic, swarm-heavy level and reveal that the Concordat is growing biological components underground.

### Objectives

1. Enter the silo network.
2. Locate the biological containment sector.
3. Destroy three Nanite Brood Nodes.
4. Survive the containment breach.
5. Reach the underground freight line.

### New enemy behavior

- Nanite Pods deploy in groups;
- support enemies retreat behind stronger units;
- swarm enemies attack from vents and ceiling openings;
- environmental growth can block previously open routes.

## E2M4 — The Architect’s Foundry

### Story purpose

Introduce The Architect as the intelligence that turns industrial facilities into gate structures.

### Objectives

1. Enter the central foundry.
2. Disable the geometry stabilizers.
3. Survive the shifting arena.
4. Expose The Architect’s core.
5. Defeat The Architect.

### Boss: The Architect

The Architect does not fight as a conventional creature. It changes the combat space.

#### Phase 1 — Assembly

- creates cover and elevated platforms;
- deploys Phalanx Troopers;
- opens and closes combat lanes;
- exposes a core after each stabilizer is destroyed.

#### Phase 2 — Reconfiguration

- rotates sections of the arena;
- creates temporary void zones;
- spawns Razor-Stalkers from unfinished frames;
- forces the player to keep moving.

#### Phase 3 — Collapse

- the foundry begins to fall apart;
- The Architect loses control of the arena;
- the player must destroy three exposed core conduits;
- final escape sequence begins immediately after victory.

### Boss dialogue

**ARCHITECT:** Structure is suffering delayed by geometry.

**ROOK:** You built a machine out of a city.

**ARCHITECT:** I built a future out of a failure.

**ROOK:** Then fail with it.

## E2M5 — Railgun Causeway

Rook crosses an automated weapons platform carrying gate components to the orbital launch facility.

Gameplay focus:

- long exterior traversal;
- moving rail platforms;
- heavy enemy artillery;
- timed cover transitions;
- optional sabotage of the railgun network.

## E2M6 — Reactor Spine

Rook enters the central reactor and must stabilize three energy cores while the facility is being converted around them.

Gameplay focus:

- circular arena structure;
- reactor heat management;
- enemies entering from multiple vertical levels;
- temporary shutdown windows;
- high-risk shortcuts.

## E2M7 — Launch Facility

Rook reaches the orbital tether launch complex and discovers that the facility is preparing an automated launch of the final gate anchor.

Gameplay focus:

- large military hangar;
- vehicle and machinery cover;
- elite enemy squads;
- countdown event;
- launch tower ascent.

## E2M8 — Tether Ignition

Rook must activate the orbital tether while the Concordat attempts to overload the launch facility.

### Final encounter

- defend three ignition consoles;
- rotate through vertical platforms;
- destroy incoming anchor components;
- survive a heavy elite wave;
- enter the tether launch capsule.

### Episode ending

**MARA:** Tether ignition is stable.

**SOREN:** Stable is not the word I would use.

**ROOK:** It launches or it explodes.

**MARA:** Those are not the only outcomes.

**ROOK:** They are the useful ones.

The capsule launches toward the orbital station, beginning Episode 3.
