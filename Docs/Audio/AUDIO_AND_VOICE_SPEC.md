# Audio and Voice Specification

## Audio pillars

1. Combat impact must feel immediate and physical.
2. Every enemy must be identifiable by sound before it is visible.
3. Music must support movement and escalation without masking gameplay cues.
4. Radio dialogue must remain intelligible during combat.
5. Sound should reinforce the cyan/magenta industrial identity without becoming noisy.

## Sound categories

### Weapons

- Valkyrie Arc-Carbine fire;
- impact on metal, concrete, armor, organic surface, and energy shield;
- empty-ammo feedback;
- weapon pickup;
- upgrade station;
- Singularity Disruptor charge, pull, implosion, and cooldown.

### Player

- footsteps by material;
- jump, land, slide, and hard landing;
- armor impact;
- health damage;
- low-health warning;
- death and revive;
- interaction feedback.

### Enemies

- Razor-Stalker idle, alert, lunge, miss, stagger, and death;
- Phalanx shield activate, shield hit, shield break, weapon fire, and death;
- boss phase transitions;
- Nanite Pod deployment;
- Aero-Wraith movement and attack.

### Environment

- electrical hum;
- distant machinery;
- relay startup;
- doors and locks;
- dimensional rifts;
- alarms;
- reactor heat;
- orbital station decompression.

## Music system

Music layers:

- exploration bed;
- tension layer;
- combat rhythm;
- elite layer;
- boss layer;
- victory or transition layer.

The system must support crossfades and intensity changes based on encounter state, remaining enemies, player health, and boss phase.

## Voice direction

- Mara: controlled, technical, gradually more personal.
- Rook: concise, dry, restrained.
- Soren: intelligent, anxious, morally conflicted.
- Vael: calm military certainty.
- The Architect: layered synthetic voice with precise timing.
- Xylia: quiet, articulate, never hysterical.

## Recording requirements

Record English master lines first. Each line requires:

- stable dialogue key;
- speaker;
- subtitle text;
- estimated duration;
- emotional direction;
- priority;
- interruption rules;
- localized text status;
- audio file status.
